# Law texts

`pdf/` holds the official PDFs. `*.txt` files are generated from them with `npm run convert-laws` (in `server/`),
which removes only page furniture (cover page, contents, running headers, footers, page numbers). The law text is
not edited.

| File | Law | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `constitution-chapter-4.txt` | Constitution of the Republic of Uganda, 1995, Chapter Four only | ULII, as at 31 Dec 2023 |
| `landlord-and-tenant-act-2022.txt` | Landlord and Tenant Act, 2022 (Act 9 of 2022, Cap. 238) | ULII, as at 31 Dec 2023 |
| `land-act-cap-236.txt` | Land Act, Cap. 236 (Act 16 of 1998) | ULII, as at 31 Dec 2023 |
| `access-to-information-act-2005.txt` | Access to Information Act, 2005 (Act 6 of 2005, Cap. 95) | ULII, as at 31 Dec 2023 |
| `employment-amendment-act-2026.txt` | Employment (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act 10 of 2026) | Uganda Gazette, Acts Supplement No. 6, 5 June 2026 |
| `employment-act-cap-226.txt` | Employment Act, Cap. 226 (Act 6 of 2006) | ULII, as at 31 Dec 2023 |

Where the Employment (Amendment) Act, 2026 changes a section of the Employment Act, the amendment applies.

ULII texts come from [ulii.org](https://ulii.org) and Laws.Africa under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
